## Vision Statement

Transform the navigation and induction experience within the university campus, empowering students with an intelligent and autonomous tool that eliminates disorientation and democratizes quick access to institutional information.

## Vision Board

| Target Audience | Needs and Problems | The Product | Business Value and Objectives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| • Freshmen who are unaware of the geographical distribution of faculties and departments.<br><br>• Regular undergraduate and graduate students who need to locate administrative offices or check service schedules.<br><br>• University administrative staff.<br><br>• Visitors, applicants, and exchange students requiring temporary orientation on campus. | • The size of the campus makes finding a specific building, laboratory, or gate frustrating for new students.<br><br>• Administrative office or cafeteria schedules are scattered across PDFs, outdated websites, or social media.<br><br>• Traditional static 2D maps do not provide clear step-by-step "how to get there" guidance or friendly visual feedback.<br><br>• Certain university locations on Google Maps are outdated and do not cover the entire campus. | • 3D campus map rendering using Mapbox, with a customized avatar system to track the user's location in real-time.<br><br>• Automatic routing of the shortest or most efficient path from point A to point B within the facilities.<br><br>• Integration of an Artificial Intelligence chatbot that understands natural language.<br><br>• Centralized repository fed with official documents to ensure the AI provides accurate responses without "hallucinations".<br><br>• Frontend development in React Native to ensure compatibility with Android and iOS, along with a high-performance backend. | • Significantly reduce the time students waste looking for classrooms or offices, improving punctuality and the university experience.<br><br>• Demonstrate the feasibility of integrating Generative AI into mobile devices without compromising hardware performance.<br><br>• Deliver a backend architecture that allows future integration of new modules simply by adding more documents to the vector database. |
